## SUBJUNCTIVE

The **subjunctive** is a possible value of [mode](modus.md). It denotes with the [present](praesens.md) and [perfect](perfectum.md) tenses that the author/speaker requests, wishes, or hopes for the given [verb](actus.md) to be performed or to have been performed; and with the [imperfect](infectum.md) and [pluperfect](plusquamperfectum.md) tenses that (s)he acknowledges its possibility. The subjunctive mode does not exist in the [future](futurum.md) and [future-perfect](exigendum.md) tenses.
